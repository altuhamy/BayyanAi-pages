Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

Bayyan AI ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect through bayyanai.com, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you submit through our enquiry form: your name, email address, company name, the service you are interested in, and the message you write.
  • Basic technical data generated by our hosting provider and content delivery network, such as IP address and request logs, for security and abuse prevention.

We do not use advertising cookies and we do not sell personal data.

Why we collect it

  • To respond to your enquiry and provide the services you request.
  • To keep our website secure and prevent spam or abuse.
  • To meet our legal and accounting obligations.

Legal basis

Where the GDPR or UAE data protection law applies, we process enquiry data on the basis of your consent and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, and on the basis of contract where we provide services to you.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf, including website hosting and content delivery, and our form-handling provider. They only receive the data needed to perform their function. We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

Retention

Enquiry messages are kept for as long as needed to handle your request and any resulting engagement, and then for a reasonable period for record-keeping. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal data, or object to our processing of it. To make a request, email us at info@bayyanai.com.

Security

This website is served over HTTPS. Access to enquiry data is limited to the people who need it to respond to you.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
